# Halfie Terms of Use

[Türkçe](kosullar) · [Support](./)

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

1. **Service:** Halfie lets two people share lists, notes, events and messages with each other. It is not a social network.
2. **Account:** You create your account with Sign in with Apple and are responsible for keeping your Apple account secure.
   You must be 18 or older.
3. **Pairing:** Only pair with someone you know and trust. Pairing requires both people's consent, and either person
   can end it at any time. When pairing ends, the shared data is deleted from your phone.
4. **Emergency alert:** The emergency alert is **not an emergency service**. Whether it reaches your partner depends
   on the internet connection, notification settings and your partner's phone. In a life-threatening situation,
   **call your local emergency number.**
5. **Halfie+ and gifts:** Halfie+ is an auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription charged to your Apple account.
   It renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Cancellation and refunds follow
   Apple's rules (iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions). One partner's subscription is also unlocked for the
   other partner while the pairing lasts; when the pairing ends it stops for the former partner, and the subscriber
   keeps sharing with a new partner. One-time gifts (e.g. Rose) stay unlocked on the account that bought them.
   Purchases are also subject to
   [Apple's Standard End User License Agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).
6. **Free plan:** 2 hearts a day; Shopping, Events and 1 extra list. When Halfie+ ends, lists created later are not
   deleted but locked; they unlock again with Halfie+, or you can delete them.
7. **Prohibited use:** Sharing illegal content, accessing another person's account without permission, or overloading
   the service is prohibited.
8. **Content:** What you share belongs to you. Because it is end-to-end encrypted, Halfie cannot see or moderate it.
9. **Liability:** The service is provided "as is"; we do not guarantee that it will be uninterrupted or error-free.
10. **Deleting your account:** You can delete your account in the app at any time.
11. **Governing law:** The laws of the Republic of Türkiye.
12. **Contact:** [official.mediatv.tr@gmail.com](mailto:official.mediatv.tr@gmail.com)
